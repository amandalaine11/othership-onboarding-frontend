Honorary Agreements

Please read the full agreement before signing.

Honouring Othership's Hard Work. And Honouring You.

This agreement is intended to do two things:

  • Bring awareness to the time, energy and financial resources Othership has invested to create a world-class experience
  • Create clarity around the Guide <> Othership relationship, our mutual responsibilities, and how the company plans to invest in — and honour — you

Some rules of the road

  • Once onboarding is complete you will gain access to a significant amount of proprietary material. This information will not be shared anywhere beyond your work at Othership.
  • You will not "represent" Othership outside the physical house without getting approval from your Lead Guide.
  • We will honour you and give you the tools to grow within Othership.

The Othership Vision

Othership is changing the landscape of mental health by helping people learn to regulate their emotions in a community driven way that is fun and habit forming.

What Makes Othership Unique

  • Emotional Regulation Classes — first of its kind in the sauna and ice bath.
  • Combination of Elements — song choice, lighting changes, essential oils, script and movement.
  • Social Experiences — building trusted community through the hot and cold.
  • Othership Breathwork Style — rollercoaster breath style following a DJ set.

Privacy & Confidentiality

Required reading before going floor-ready.

At Othership, we hold space for deeply personal experiences. As a Guide, you are trusted with sensitive information and intimate moments. This trust is sacred.

Your Responsibilities

  • Everything shared in the space stays in the space.
  • You may not share guest experiences, names, or personal details outside of Othership without explicit consent.
  • Proprietary training materials and class scripts are confidential.
  • Photography and video of guests requires explicit consent.
  • Social media posts referencing Othership must be approved by your Lead Guide before posting.

Breaches of Confidentiality

Any breach may result in immediate offboarding. When in doubt, check with your Lead Guide first.

Code of Conduct

Othership's standards for every guide, every shift.

Being a Guide at Othership is a privilege. These standards exist to protect the experience — for Journeyers, for your crew, and for yourself.

Professionalism

  • Arrive on time, changed, and ready to punch in at your scheduled start.
  • Communicate proactively if you need to drop a shift.
  • Your phone stays out of the session space during shifts.

Guest Experience

  • Every Journeyer deserves a world-class experience, every single time.
  • Issues with guests are escalated to the Ship Captain.

Safety

  • Your CPR/AED certification must be valid at all times while working on the floor.
  • Know the emergency protocols for your location before your first shift.
